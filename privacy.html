Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. The English text is the binding version; a Turkish translation is available with the language button above.

Short version: CastCar requires no account to use. Your playlists, logins, favorites and files stay on your phone and are sent only to the servers you enter yourself. If you optionally sign in with your email to link or restore a purchase, we store your email and an account ID on our own server. We use analytics and crash reports that do not contain your playlists, logins or email, and the free version shows ads on the phone.

1. Who we are

CastCar is developed by Sercan Laçın ("we"). Contact: lacinsercan6@gmail.com. This policy covers the CastCar app for iPhone and CarPlay, its screen-mirroring extension and this website.

2. What CastCar is

CastCar is a player. It comes with no channels or content and has no content partners. You add your own sources: M3U/M3U8 playlists, Xtream, Jellyfin, Emby and Plex accounts, video files on your device, websites in the in-app browser, and screen mirroring.

3. Data that stays on your device

Your playlists, channel lists, account details for your sources (such as Xtream, Jellyfin, Emby or Plex logins), favorites, resume positions, imported files, browser history and settings are stored only on your device. They are sent only to the server you entered, when the app loads your list or plays a stream. We do not receive, store or see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

4. Support code

On first launch the app creates a random support code (for example CC-7K3M-9QXA), shown in Settings. It is used as the user identifier for crash reports, analytics and purchase records, so that we can help you when you write to us. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple Account.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how CastCar is used and to fix problems. Collected: app and feature usage (for example which screens are used, whether a car is connected, the car screen type and resolution), playback quality (start time, interruptions, network type, video resolution and codec, player engine), the type of sources you use and how many lists you have (a number, not the lists), purchase screen activity, device model, operating system, app version, the support code and crash reports.

These events never include your playlist addresses, channel or video names, usernames or passwords, or your precise location. Crash reports include recent technical log lines, which may contain the domain name or IP address of a stream server you use.

6. Purchases and optional account sign-in

CastCar Premium is sold through Apple's App Store and on our website (processed by Stripe). We never receive or store your full payment card details. If you choose to sign in with your email address (on the web or in Settings → Account in the app) to link your purchase across devices, we store your email address, a random account identifier and sign-in session records on our own server in Germany. The one-time sign-in link is sent to that address through Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES, Frankfurt region) from noreply@playgrator.com; we do not track opens or clicks in these emails and do not use your address for marketing. The account identifier is shared with RevenueCat only to link your purchases. Your email address and account identifier are never sent to Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account); this permanently removes your email address and sessions from our server.

We use RevenueCat to record purchases and subscription status. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription information from Apple or Stripe, the support code, your account UID (if signed in), the Firebase app-instance identifier, an Apple Search Ads attribution token (to know whether the install came from an App Store ad), and general app information such as the type of sources you use, how many lists you have added, the type of car screen and where the purchase screen was opened. It does not receive playlist addresses, channel names or logins. Even if you delete your app account, transaction and invoice records in Stripe and RevenueCat are retained as required by applicable tax and accounting laws.

7. Advertising

The free version shows Google AdMob ads on the phone, never on the car screen. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address and data about the ads you see and tap. If you allow tracking in the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt, this data may be used for personalized ads; otherwise ads are non-personalized. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent message. Premium removes ads.

8. Location

If you allow location access, CastCar uses your location for turn-by-turn navigation (Apple Maps services) and to show your speed and local weather on the car home screen. Speed is calculated on the device. For the weather, your location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service. CastCar does not send your location to our servers or to analytics. Location in the background is used only while navigation guidance is active.

9. Camera, microphone and photos

  • Camera: used only to scan the QR code on the car screen to pair with CarPlay. Images are processed on the device and not saved or sent.
  • Microphone: used only when a website you open in the in-app browser asks for it (for example voice search).
  • Photos: CastCar only adds an image to your library when you choose to save one from the browser. It does not read your photo library.

10. Car screen, browser and mirroring

Screen mirroring travels locally between your phone and the car; nothing is uploaded. The in-app browser connects directly to the websites you open; those websites may use their own cookies and collect data under their own policies. Browser history and site data are stored on your device and can be cleared in the app.

11. Content from our website and Apple

The app downloads a public channel-logo index from castcar.playgrator.com and checks for new versions using Apple's public App Store lookup. These requests contain no personal data. Like any web server, ours may keep standard access logs (IP address, time, requested file) for a short time for security.

12. Diagnostics log

CastCar keeps a technical log on your device (Settings → Diagnostics log) to help solve playback problems. It stays on your device unless you choose to attach it to a problem report. It may contain stream server domain names and channel or site names, but no passwords.

13. Children

CastCar is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

14. Your choices

  • Tracking and ads: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or buy Premium to remove ads.
  • Location: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → CastCar.
  • Data in the app: delete sources, favorites, files and browser data in the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on the device.
  • Data held by our providers: write to lacinsercan6@gmail.com with your support code and we will request deletion of the analytics, crash and purchase records linked to it.

15. This website

castcar.playgrator.com uses no analytics, no advertising cookies and no tracking. It only remembers your language choice in your browser.

16. Changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and show the date above.
